N06 / Admissions / Reviewed individually

Applications are open.

Every application is read by a person, not filtered by a form. We are assessing fit in both directions, because three years is a serious commitment for you and for us.

01 / Process

Four steps, start to finish.

01

Submit your application

Background, experience, and a short statement covering why this programme and why now. Takes about twenty minutes.

02

Brief interview

In person or by video call. A conversation, not an exam. We are checking fit in both directions.

03

Offer and enrolment

If it is a fit, you receive an offer and secure your place in the cohort with a deposit.

04

Onboarding

Platform access, cohort introductions, reading list and intensive dates ahead of the Year One start.

02 / Requirements

What you need to apply.

Age

18 and over

Jurisdiction dependent for cannabis-related study. We will confirm the position for your country during the interview.

Language

English

The programme is delivered in English, including all coursework, assessment and in-person intensives.

Background

No experience required

Most graduates arrive with none. A science background is recommended but not required for the Medical and Clinical Liaison track.

Commitment

Three years, in person

You must be able to travel for a minimum of three in-person intensives per year. Dates are published well in advance.

Before you apply

Worth knowing

This is a three-year programme with real assessment and mandatory in-person attendance. It is not a self-paced video library, and it is not designed to be completed casually alongside a full unrelated workload.

  • Coursework runs continuously through each year
  • Minimum three intensives per year, in person
  • Assessment at the end of each module
  • Capstone project in Year Three

Questions about eligibility, funding or timing are welcome before you apply. Read the FAQ or get in touch.
