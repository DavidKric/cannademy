Field Notes / 01

Most courses teach the plant. We teach you to read it.

Harvest timing, potency, and quality all come down to details this small: a single gland turning from clear to amber. That's why trichome staging gets real hours, hands-on, under magnification, on a working flowering-room floor. Not a slide deck.
